This site ran on Hexo for years with exactly one post, from 2019, that said "This is my first post".

It now runs on [Astro](https://astro.build/). Content is Markdown, GitHub Actions builds it, and the
browser gets plain HTML. At two or three posts a year the only requirement that matters is that the
site still works when I come back to it.

More to come.
